Let's assign some stats!

You may assign your stats as you please. That being said, here are some guidelines.

Each stat defaults to an 'average' one, costing zero points. For each degree above it costs first one point, than three points, and finally six points for the highest tier. Likewise, for each tier below average it is -1, -3, and -6 attribute points. For a moderate experience try to not go above 15-20 positive points! (Though really this is just guesswork!)

5. Mhm, well what about endurance? How much can you take?

8. I see! Learned lots? Would you call yourself experienced?

9. Fair enough! Any talent for noticing things? Observation?

11. And what about socially? Are you persuasive? Good at convincing?

13. Ask yourself a question. 'Do I feel lucky?' Well, do ya, punk?
